# Lightworks Pro — 90-Second Promotional Video Script
**Version 1.0 · May 2026**
**Total runtime: ~90 seconds**

---

## Production Notes

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 (1920×1080) |
| Colour palette | Deep navy `#0A1628`, electric blue `#1E6FD9`, white `#FFFFFF`, gold accent `#F5A623` |
| Font | Inter or Montserrat — bold headers, regular body |
| Music | Cinematic corporate — builds from subtle ambient to confident resolve; no lyrics |
| Voiceover | Professional, warm, measured pace — not rushed |
| SFX | Subtle soft chime on each feature reveal; gentle keyboard tap on hotkey callouts |

## Scene 1 — The Problem (0:00 – 0:12)

**Duration:** 12 seconds

### Visuals
- Black screen fades to dark navy.
- A single glowing cursor blinks in the centre of an empty screen — no icons, no windows, just the cursor.
- Slow-motion stock footage: a professional at a desk, hands resting, a screen in front of them they cannot see. Colleagues move around them — emails piling up, meeting notifications flashing — all invisible noise.
- Subtle text fades in, white on navy:

> **"Every day, millions of professionals navigate a digital world built for sight."**

### Narration
> *"Every day, millions of professionals rely on digital tools to stay connected, to lead meetings, to send emails — and every tool assumes they can see a screen."*

### Tone
Quiet. Serious. Sets stakes.

---

## Scene 2 — The Reveal (0:12 – 0:22)

**Duration:** 10 seconds

### Visuals
- Cut to black.
- A single voice waveform pulses — electric blue on black — like a heartbeat.
- The Lightworks Pro logo fades in from the waveform. Clean. Bold. Professional.
- Tagline appears beneath:

> **"Your voice. Your workspace. Your pace."**

- Music lifts: a confident, forward-moving corporate score.

### Narration
> *"Lightworks Pro changes that. A voice-first productivity assistant, built from the ground up for blind and visually impaired professionals — works silently alongside your screen reader, never against it."*

### SFX
Soft chime as logo appears.

---

## Scene 3 — Core Architecture (0:22 – 0:35)

**Duration:** 13 seconds

### Visuals
- Animated diagram: clean line-art icons appear one by one — a microphone, a calendar, an email envelope, a Teams/Zoom logo — all connected to a central node labelled "Lightworks Pro."
- Below the diagram, a slim bar pulses: **JAWS | NVDA | Fusion | ZoomText** — labelled "Works alongside your AT. Always."
- No visual clutter. Icons are large, high-contrast, white on navy.

### Narration
> *"Running silently as a background service, Lightworks Pro connects directly to Microsoft 365 — your email, your calendar, your meetings — and delivers everything through your system's natural voice, the same one your screen reader uses."*

---

## Scene 4 — Feature 1: Meetings (0:35 – 0:50)

**Duration:** 15 seconds

### Visuals
- Split animation: a calendar icon on the left; a voice waveform on the right.
- Text callouts appear as the narrator speaks them, gold accent colour, appearing with a soft chime:

  - `"10 minutes before a meeting"` → shield icon (alert, no action needed)
  - `"5 minutes before"` → shield icon
  - `"At start time"` → green play icon + `"15-second auto-join countdown"`
  - `"Say CANCEL to abort"` → red stop icon

- Final frame: the Teams and Zoom logos appear side by side, subtitled "Supports Teams · Zoom · Webex · Google Meet."

### Narration
> *"Meetings? Handled. Lightworks Pro announces your next meeting at ten minutes and five minutes — no action required. At start time, it counts down and joins automatically. Just say cancel if plans change. Teams, Zoom, Webex, Google Meet — all supported."*

### SFX
Three soft chimes, one per callout.

---

## Scene 5 — Feature 2: Email Intelligence (0:50 – 1:05)

**Duration:** 15 seconds

### Visuals
- Email envelope icon centre frame. Subtle animated lines radiate outward like a pulse.
- Text callouts with chimes:

  - `"Reads your inbox — high-priority first"`
  - `"Navigate with your voice — say NEXT"`
  - `"Compose by guided wizard"`
  - `"Send or save as draft"`

- Brief animated sequence: waveform prompt → "Who are you sending to?" → contact name appears → "What is the subject?" → "Speak your message."

### Narration
> *"Email Intelligence reads your inbox to you — high-priority messages first. Navigate with your voice. When you're ready to reply or compose, a guided step-by-step wizard walks you through recipient, subject, and message. Send immediately or save as a draft — your choice, every time."*

### SFX
Soft chime on each callout.

---

## Scene 6 — Security and Trust (1:05 – 1:15)

**Duration:** 10 seconds

### Visuals
- Dark navy background. A clean padlock icon — electric blue — centre frame. Glows once.
- Text appears:

> **Notify → Query → Consent → Execute**

- Four steps animate in sequence, connected by a clean horizontal line. Each step lights up gold as the narrator names it.
- Subtitle: `"Nothing happens without your explicit voice confirmation."`

### Narration
> *"Privacy and safety are built into every action. Notify. Query. Consent. Execute. Lightworks Pro never acts without your explicit voice confirmation. No silent automation. No surprises."*

## Scene 7 — Call to Action (1:15 – 1:30)

**Duration:** 15 seconds

### Visuals
- Full-screen deep navy. Lightworks Pro logo — large, centred, confident.
- Tagline beneath:

> **"Your voice. Your workspace. Your pace."**

- Three supporting lines fade in, staggered:

  - `"Available now for Windows — Microsoft 365"`
  - `"Built for JAWS · NVDA · Fusion · ZoomText"`
  - `"Developed at Lighthouse Guild, New York"`

- Gold accent line under the logo pulses gently.
- Music peaks, then resolves to a clean, quiet end.
- Final text hold for 3 seconds: logo + tagline only.

### Narration
> *"Lightworks Pro. Available now for Windows, with Microsoft 365. Built for professionals who rely on JAWS, NVDA, Fusion, and ZoomText — and engineered to stay out of their way.*
>
> *Your voice. Your workspace. Your pace."*

## Production Recommendations

- **Tool options:** Canva Pro (template + voiceover), Adobe Premiere, CapCut Pro, or Descript (record narration → auto-animate)
- **Stock footage:** Pexels or Storyblocks — search "blind professional workspace", "accessibility technology", "voice assistant"
- **Voiceover:** Eleven Labs (AI VO) or a professional narrator; warm mid-range voice, not robotic
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound — search "corporate inspiring" or "accessible professional"; keep under -20 dB LUFS behind narration
- **Logo/assets:** Use `assets/lightworks_pro.ico` as base; export to PNG at 512×512 for video use
- **Accessibility of the video itself:** Include closed captions, high-contrast text, and an audio description track — modelling the values of the product
